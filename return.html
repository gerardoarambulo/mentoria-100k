Returns and Exchanges:

  1. 30-Day Satisfaction Guarantee: We offer a 30-day window from the date of delivery for you to return or exchange your items. If you're not completely satisfied with your purchase, you can initiate a return or exchange within this timeframe.

Return Shipping:

  • You are responsible for the cost of return shipping unless the return is due to an error on our part (e.g., you received a defective or incorrect item).

Refunds:

  • Once we receive your returned item and inspect it, we will process your refund or exchange. Refunds will be issued to the original payment method, and you can expect the funds to be credited within 7 business days.

NOTE: Please note that in the event of a return, a 25% restocking fee will be applied to cover the operational costs incurred. These costs are non-refundable, as they may vary depending on the destination country and the size of the product.

  • For hygiene and safety reasons, certain items like masks, clothing, shoes, and other wearable items cannot be returned.
  • If you received a damaged or defective item, please contact our customer support team within 7 days of receiving the item to initiate a return or exchange.
  • Keep the original packaging, as it will help protect the item during the return process.
